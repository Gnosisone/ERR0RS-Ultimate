# ERR0RS — Mission

> **ERR0RS exists to give every aspiring security professional — especially the
> ones with nothing — a teacher better than money can buy.**

ERR0RS-Ultimate is a fully local, open-source AI penetration-testing platform and
security mentor. It runs entirely on your own machine — no cloud, no data leaving
the device — so professional-grade offensive-security education is free, private,
and available to anyone, anywhere. The barrier to learning security should be
curiosity, not a tuition bill.

## The three pillars

- **Knowledge** — make professional-grade security education free and accessible
  to everyone, not just those who can pay for it.
- **Integrity** — authorized, ethical, legal testing only. We are honest about
  what the tool is, what it is not, and where its limits lie.
- **Security** — fully local and private by design. Your data, your work, and
  your targets stay yours; nothing leaves the device.

## What it is
A local AI that teaches as it works: 80+ hand-authored lessons, a retrieval-grounded
assistant, an autonomous-but-supervised kill chain, and a CFAA-aware safety gate —
all running airgapped on hardware as small as a Raspberry Pi.
