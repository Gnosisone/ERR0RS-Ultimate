Mission

Every aspiring security professional — especially the ones with nothing — deserves a teacher better than money can buy.

ERR0RS runs entirely on your own machine. No cloud, no data leaving the device. Professional-grade offensive-security education, free and private, for anyone, anywhere. The barrier to learning security should be curiosity — not a tuition bill.

Three pillars

Knowledge

Professional-grade security education, free and accessible to everyone — not just those who can pay.

Integrity

Authorized, ethical, legal testing only. Honest about what the tool is, isn't, and where its limits lie.

Security

Fully local and private by design. Your data, your work, your targets stay yours.

What it is

  • 80+ hand-authored lessons, from Linux basics to writing your own port scanner
  • A retrieval-grounded assistant that teaches as it works
  • An autonomous-but-supervised kill chain with a reasoning/parsing split
  • A CFAA-aware safety gate and tiered operator attestation
  • Runs airgapped on hardware as small as a Raspberry Pi
